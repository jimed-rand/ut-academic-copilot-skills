## 1. The 5-Technique Structural Paraphrasing Framework

True academic paraphrasing requires **simultaneously transforming sentence structure, clause order, and lexical framing** while preserving 100% of factual claims, quantitative values, and citations. Changing only 2–3 words with synonyms is **patchwriting** (a form of plagiarism flagged by Turnitin and DrillBit).

### Technique 1: Clause Re-Anchoring & Structure Transformation
Move the subordinate context, empirical constraint, regulatory basis, or scholar attribution to the front of the sentence, or merge two sequential cause-and-effect sentences using a semicolon (`;`) or colon (`:`).

- **Original (Source / Tugas 1–3)**:
  > Pemerintah Indonesia telah menerbitkan UU Nomor 27 Tahun 2022 tentang Pelindungan Data Pribadi untuk mengatur kewajiban pengendali data dalam menjaga keamanan informasi warga negara.
- **Structural Paraphrase (0% Similarity & 0% AI)**:
  > Kewajiban hukum pengendali data elektronik dalam menjamin kerahasiaan, integritas, serta yurisdiksi penyimpanan informasi warga negara diatur melalui ketentuan Pasal 13 s.d. Pasal 16 UU No. 27 Tahun 2022 tentang Pelindungan Data Pribadi (PDP).

### Technique 2: Voice Shift (Passive $\leftrightarrow$ Active Scholarly Agency)
Convert repetitive passive `di-...-kan` chains into active investigative clauses where the researcher, instrument, protocol, or scholar acts as the grammatical subject—or shift actor-focused clauses to object-focused methodological descriptions where appropriate.

- **Passive Chain (AI / Source)**:
  > Pengumpulan data dilakukan melalui wawancara mendalam dengan lima orang informan kunci. Selanjutnya, analisis data dilakukan dengan cara mereduksi transkrip wawancara.
- **Active / Methodological Agency (Paraphrased)**:
  > Peneliti menggali data primer melalui wawancara semi-terstruktur bersama lima informan kunci (*purposive sampling*); seluruh transkrip verba kemudian dikondensasi mengikuti tahapan model interaktif Miles et al. (2014).

### Technique 3: Part-of-Speech & Nominalization Conversion
Transform heavy nominalizations (`pengimplementasian`, `pendistribusian`, `pengoptimalan`) into concrete active verbs (`menerapkan`, `menyalurkan`, `mengoptimalkan`), or condense verbose relative clauses (`yang merupakan suatu mekanisme yang digunakan untuk...`) into concise prepositional or participial phrases.

- **Verbose Nominalized AI Clause**:
  > Pengimplementasian sistem enkripsi yang dilakukan secara menyeluruh merupakan langkah yang sangat penting untuk memberikan perlindungan terhadap kebocoran metadata.
- **Concrete Verbal Clause**:
  > Penerapan enkripsi *multi-end* berbasis spesifikasi XEP No. 0384 (OMEMO) menutup celah penyadapan isi pesan sekaligus meminimalkan eksposur metadata pada peladen perantara (Ermoshina et al., 2016).

### Technique 4: Substantive Citation Integration (Scholars as Agents)
Never isolate citations as decorative sentence-ending tags after generic claims (*"Hal ini sesuai dengan penelitian terdahulu (Smith, 2020)."*). Integrate scholars as active intellectual agents or group empirical findings by mechanism:

| Citation Pattern | Mechanical AI Form (Avoid) | Authentic Scholarly Form (Use) |
|---|---|---|
| **Scholars as Agents** | Menurut penelitian, kepercayaan sosial sangat penting (Putnam, 2000). | Putnam (2000) menempatkan modal kepercayaan sosial (*social trust*) sebagai fondasi partisipasi sipil. |
| **Findings as Core Content** | Banyak studi telah meneliti protokol XMPP (Ermoshina et al., 2016; Halpin et al., 2018). | Evaluasi kriptografis membuktikan bahwa ekstensi OMEMO (XEP No. 0384) pada protokol XMPP menyediakan properti *forward secrecy* dan *deniability* lintas perangkat (Ermoshina et al., 2016; Halpin et al., 2018). |
| **Critical Contrast** | Smith (2020) meneliti dampak algoritma. Jones (2021) memiliki pendapat berbeda. | Klaim korelasional Smith (2020) mengenai polarisasi algoritmik dikritisi oleh Jones (2021) karena belum mengisolasi bias seleksi mandiri (*self-selection bias*). |

### Technique 5: Multi-Source Synthesis & Clause Consolidation
Combine fragmented 15–20 word sentences into rich, 35–65 word multi-clause sentences joined by semicolons (`;`), colons (`:`), and parenthetical specifications `(mis. ...)`.

### Paraphrasing Golden Rules (What NOT to Paraphrase)
1. **Never alter numbers, sample sizes ($N$), dates, $p$-values, or statutory article numbers.**
2. **Never invent or translate standard technical protocol identifiers** (`IETF RFC No. 6120`, `XEP No. 0384`, `ISO/IEC No. 27001`, `ANOVA`, `Cronbach's alpha`).
3. **Never use AI-paraphraser slop synonyms** (*sanggup*, *obrolan*, *pemakai*, *kawakan*, *paguyuban*, *simpul*, *alur*). Choose precise disciplinary terminology instead.
4. **Target N-gram Similarity**: Ensure 6-gram and 8-gram overlap against source texts (including the student's own `Tugas 1–3`) is **$< 15\%$**.

## 2. Humanizing Academic Prose: 10 Rewriting Principles

Adapted from `humanize-academic-writing` and calibrated with our empirical DrillBit 0%-AI findings:

1. **Eliminate Mechanical Sentence-Initial Transitions**:
   - Strip robotic openers: *Moveover, Furthermore, Additionally, In addition, Selain itu, Lebih lanjut, Di samping itu, Selanjutnya, Oleh karena itu, Dengan demikian, Hal ini menunjukkan bahwa*.
   - Connect ideas implicitly through cause-and-effect semicolons (`;`), content-driven demonstratives (*Temuan empiris pada Tabel 2.1 tersebut...*, *Ketimpangan topologi ini...*), or direct thematic progression.
2. **Replace Abstract Scaffolding with Concrete Specificity**:
   - Delete hollow filler: *"berbagai aspek"*, *"banyak faktor"*, *"memainkan peran yang sangat penting"*, *"dalam konteks ini"*, *"perlu dicatat bahwa"*, *"various aspects"*, *"plays a crucial role"*.
   - Replace every abstraction with the exact variables, regulations (`UU No. 27 Tahun 2022`), protocol standards (`RFC No. 6120`), scholar names (`Ermoshina et al., 2016`), and empirical metrics.
3. **Calibrate Scholarly Hedging**:
   - **Zero hedging** on what the study proposes, defines, or directly measures (*"Penelitian ini menganalisis..."*, *"Latensi turun sebesar 38%."*).
   - **Single, scoped epistemic hedge** on interpretations and generalizations (*"Pola ini mengindikasikan bahwa..."*, *"Temuan tersebut berpotensi berbeda pada beban kerja tulis-intensif."*).
   - **Zero double hedges** (*"mungkin berpotensi dapat..."*, *"seems to suggest that X might possibly..."*).
4. **Vary Paragraph Openings**:
   - Never start three consecutive paragraphs with `"Penelitian ini..."`, `"Berdasarkan..."`, or `"The [noun] [verb]..."`.
   - Alternate across: (a) empirical problem/phenomenon, (b) statutory or technical specification anchor, (c) scholar/theoretical contrast, (d) methodological scope.
5. **High Syntactic Burstiness via Clause-Dense Semicolon/Colon Architecture + Period-Space Circuit-Breakers**:
   - Combine 35–65 word multi-clause sentences (using `;`, `:`, and `/` technical pairs) with natural period-space splits (`No.`, `et al.`, `mis.`, `s.d.`, scholar initials) so `CV >= 0.55` and `max_unbroken_run <= 4` without ever inserting artificial 6–12 word LLM signpost sentences.
6. **Balance Active and Passive Voice**:
   - Keep passive voice below 35–40% of total clauses. Use active voice for researcher decisions, theoretical arguments, and empirical findings.
7. **Terse Colon Format for Definitions & Instrument Lists**:
   - In numbered/lettered definitions, variables, and instrument modules, use `**Label**: <Subject-less active verb/noun phrase>` (`: Menelaah...`, `: Mengukur...`, `: Terdiri atas...`) with distinct opening verbs per item.
8. **Zero Em-Dashes (`—`)**:
   - Never use `—` (or spaced ` -- `) in Indonesian academic submissions; use commas, parentheses, colons, or semicolons.
9. **Discipline-Specific Theoretical Grounding**:
   - Anchor claims in named disciplinary frameworks (e.g., *Path Dependence* [W. B. Arthur, 1989], *Technology-Organization-Environment / TOE* [Tornatzky & Fleischer, 1990], *Street-Level Bureaucracy* [Lipsky, 2010], *Case Study Design* [Robert K. Yin, 2018]).
10. **Concrete, Non-Apologetic Limitations**:
    - State boundaries factually (*"Evaluasi dibatasi pada dua topologi peladen...; pengujian beban lintas benua belum dicakup."*) rather than using generic AI boilerplate (*"Meskipun penelitian ini menjanjikan, penelitian lebih lanjut sangat diperlukan di masa depan."*).

## 3. Wikipedia "Signs of AI Writing" Audit Catalog

Derived from the `humanizer` skill (based on Wikipedia's *Signs of AI writing* guide):

| AI Writing Sign | Typical LLM Manifestation (EN / ID) | Remediation Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Inflated Symbolism & Importance** | *"stands as a testament to"*, *"pivotal moment"*, *"menjadi bukti nyata"*, *"tonggak sejarah yang sangat krusial"*, *"tidak dapat dipungkiri lagi"* | Delete rhetorical puffery; state the empirical measurement, statutory mandate, or historical date directly. |
| **2. Superficial `-ing` / `yang ...` Tacking** | Trailing clauses like *"...highlighting the importance of X, fostering Y, and ensuring Z"* or *"...yang menunjukkan pentingnya X serta memberikan kontribusi signifikan bagi Y"* | Delete the generic trailing commentary or replace it with the concrete operational outcome. |
| **3. Vague Attributions (Weasel Words)** | *"Experts argue"*, *"Observers have cited"*, *"Banyak ahli berpendapat"*, *"Penelitian terdahulu membuktikan"* (without citation) | Replace with exact APA 7th citations (`Author et al., Year`) and specific empirical findings. |
| **4. Negative Parallelisms** | *"Not only X, but also Y"*, *"It's not just about X; it's about Y"*, *"Tidak hanya sekadar X, melainkan juga Y"* (repeated across sections) | State the dual relationship directly using concrete conjunctions or semicolons. |
| **5. Rule-of-Three Adjective/Noun Cloning** | Mechanical triplets in every sentence (*"cepat, aman, dan andal"*, *"efektif, efisien, dan berkelanjutan"*) | Keep only the empirically measured attributes and specify how each attribute is operationalized. |
| **6. Copula Avoidance & Pompous Verbs** | Replacing simple *is / adalah* with *"serves as"*, *"functions as"*, *"merupakan suatu bentuk manifestasi dari"* | Cap `merupakan` at $< 1.0$/1k words; use direct active verbs or concise colon definitions. |
| **7. Em-Dash & Bold-Header Bullet Spam** | Multiple `—` per paragraph; every paragraph converted into a 5-bullet list with bold single-word headers | Use continuous prose for background/synthesis paragraphs, and reserve terse colon lists strictly for formal definitions, research questions, and methodological units. |

## 4. Disciplinary Writing Conventions (Social Sciences, Public Admin, IS/IT)

| Discipline / UT Faculty | Core Theoretical & Methodological Anchors | Preferred Style & Evidence Norms |
|---|---|---|
| **Ilmu Administrasi Negara / Pemerintahan (FHISIP)** | Kebijakan Publik (Dunn, Dye, Grindle), Birokrasi Jalanan (Lipsky), *Good/Dynamic Governance* (Neo & Chen), SPBE (`Perpres No. 95 Tahun 2018`) | Anchor arguments in statutory hierarchy (`UU No.`, `PP No.`, `Perpres No.`, `PermenPAN-RB No.`); contrast normative regulatory mandates against empirical field implementation (*das Sollen* vs. *das Sein*). |
| **Sistem Informasi / Ilmu Komputer / Sains Data (FST)** | Spesifikasi protokol & standar teknis (`IETF RFC No.`, `XEP No.`, `ISO/IEC No. 27001`, `NIST`), TOE Framework, TAM/UTAUT, *Design Science Research* (Hevner et al.) | Specify exact architectural metrics (latency in ms, memory footprint in MB, encryption ciphers, S2S/C2S topology, `CPU/RAM` constraints) and regulatory compliance (`UU No. 27 Tahun 2022` / UU PDP). |
| **Sosiologi / Ilmu Komunikasi / Antropologi (FHISIP)** | Teori Praktik/Habitus (Bourdieu), Jaringan Sosial (Granovetter, Castells), Konstruksi Sosial (Berger & Luckmann), *Thick Description* (Geertz), Studi Kasus (Robert K. Yin) | Emphasize actor agency, power asymmetry, digital sovereignty (*Couture & Toupin, 2019*), and qualitative trustworthiness (triangulasi sumber, teknik, dan waktu; Miles et al., 2014). |

| **Pendidikan / FKIP** | Konstruktivisme (Vygotsky, Piaget), *Cognitive Load Theory* (Sweller), PTK (Kemmis & McTaggart), Kuasi-Eksperimen (Shadish et al.) | Connect pedagogical interventions to measurable learning outcomes ($M, SD$, Cohen's $d$, N-Gain) and classroom implementation constraints. |

## 5. Academic Proofreading & Grammar Checklist (Indonesian & English)

### A. Section-by-Section Tense & Modality Convention
| Section | English Tense Convention | Indonesian Aspect / Temporal Marker Convention |
|---|---|---|
| **Abstract / Ringkasan** | Past tense for methods/results (*"We analyzed..."*); Present tense for conclusions (*"These findings indicate..."*) | Direct past/completed framing for empirical studies; prospective/investigative framing for proposals (*"Proposal ini mengkaji..."*) |
| **Bab I: Pendahuluan** | Present tense for established facts; Present perfect for literature trends (*"Prior studies have examined..."*) | Present factual statements backed by current data/regulations; avoid speculative *"akan sangat membantu"* |
| **Bab II: Tinjauan Pustaka** | Past tense for specific prior experiments (*"Ermoshina et al. (2016) evaluated..."*); Present tense for enduring theories | Active scholarly verbs: *"Ermoshina et al. (2016) menguji..."*, *"Arthur (1989) merumuskan konsep path dependence sebagai..."* |
| **Bab III: Metode Penelitian** | Future/Prospective in proposals; Past tense in completed reports (*"Data were collected..."*) | Clear operational steps (*"Peneliti menetapkan lima informan kunci melalui purposive sampling..."*, *"Analisis data mengikuti model interaktif Miles et al. (2014)..."*) |

### B. Syntactic & Punctuation Audit Rules
1. **Subject–Predicate Completeness**: In Indonesian, never begin a main independent clause with a preposition (*Bagi, Untuk, Dalam, Pada, Dengan*) without a comma separating the introductory prepositional phrase from the explicit grammatical Subject (*Subjek*) and Predicate (*Predikat*).
2. **Oxford / Serial Comma (PUEBI)**: In Indonesian lists of three or more items, always place a comma before the final conjunction (*"A, B, dan C"* or *"X, Y, serta Z"*).
3. **Number Formatting**:
   - Spell out numbers one through nine (`satu` s.d. `sembilan` / `one` to `nine`) in general running prose; use numerals for `10` and above, percentages (`15%`), measurements (`128 MB`, `50 ms`), statutory numbers (`UU No. 27 Tahun 2022`), and table/section labels (`Tabel 2.1`, `Bab III`).
   - Never start a sentence with a bare Arabic numeral; spell it out or re-anchor the sentence.
4. **Acronym First-Use Rule**: Define every acronym at its first occurrence in the main text: *Extensible Messaging and Presence Protocol* (XMPP), *Sistem Pemerintahan Berbasis Elektronik* (SPBE), *Pelindungan Data Pribadi* (PDP). Thereafter, use the bare acronym consistently.

### B. Foreign Loanwords That MUST Be Italicized (`*...*`) in Indonesian Text
